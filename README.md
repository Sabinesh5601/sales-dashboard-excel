# Sales Dashboard

An interactive Excel dashboard that analyzes sales, quantity and profit across product categories, regions, months, customers and products.

## About the Project
This project is an interactive Excel sales dashboard built to track business performance. It shows total sales, quantity and profit, and breaks them down by product category, region, month, customer and product. Slicers for region, segment and order year let users filter the data, and a separate table highlights products that sell well but make a loss.


## Tools Used
Microsoft Excel (PivotTables, charts, slicers)

## Dashboard Features
- KPI cards for Total Sales, Total Quantity and Total Profit
- Profit by Category, Sales by Region and Sales by Month charts
- Top 10 Customers by Sales and Top 5 Products by Sales
- A table of products with high sales but low (negative) profit
- Slicers to filter by Region, Segment and Order Year

## Key Insights
- Total sales are 2,297,200.64, with 37,873 units sold and a total profit of 286,396.47, an overall profit margin of about 12.5%.
- Technology (145,455.33) and Office Supplies (122,490.04) generate almost all of the profit. Furniture contributes only 18,451.10, so it is the weakest category.
- The West region has the largest share of sales (32%), followed by East (29%), Central (22%) and South (17%).
- Sales are highest in the last quarter, peaking in November (352,461.01), with December (325,293.52) and September (307,649.91) also strong. February is the lowest month (59,751.25).
- The Canon imageCLASS 2200 Advanced Copier is the top product by sales (61,599.83), well ahead of the second product (27,453.38).
- The top customer is Sean Miller (25,043.07), followed by Tamara Chand (19,052.22).
- Several high-selling products make a loss, so they need a review of pricing and discounts.
